## Panel and spacing

The reference is a matte/textured **black panel**, with a curved upper edge, visible fasteners, dark gauge faces and mostly dark bezels. The separate pressure gauge's chrome ring is the standout bright rim. The model already constructs black panel plates, but every ordinary round instrument currently receives the same `ck_bezel` tube geometry/material. Give the new pressure gauge its own chrome treatment; a grey ring baked into its face would not produce that reflective appearance.

Six main instruments occupy one row, not five. Their photo centres slope gently upward to the right due to viewpoint: do not reproduce the image's y-pixel slope as a deliberate staircase in aircraft coordinates. Relative horizontal centre spacing, divided by the ASI's apparent 100 px diameter, is approximately **1.54, 1.31, 1.05, 1.05, 1.06**. These are composition guides (E), not an orthographic blueprint. Preserve a common aircraft-space centreline initially and validate the full arrangement from the front-seat eye. Retain three large instruments (ALT/ASI/TACH) and three smaller instruments (centre/combined oil/separate oil pressure). Do not simply squeeze the missing gauge into the current combined-to-tach gap.

## New oil pressure scale

`gl-dial-oilpress.png` uses the existing `base`, `tick`, `label`, `arc` helpers and palette, generic text **OIL PRESS / PSI**, no brand and no baked needle. `OILP2`: 0–120 psi over 300 degrees, `ang0=-150°` (7 o'clock); absolute angle = -150 + 2.5 × pressure. Zero relative rotation at zero psi. Tick spacing 5 psi, numbers every 20 psi. Yellow 25–60, green 60–90, red radials 25 and 115 psi. These are the requested typical Lycoming markings, estimates (E); they do not certify Ty's aircraft-specific limitations. Existing OILP/OILT maps remain unchanged.

## New compass texture contract

- **Moving card:** `gl-front-compass-band.png`, 2048 × 160 RGB, horizontal repeat. Full 360° revolution; **5.6888889 pixels/degree**, or **0.17578125°/pixel**. Reverse-sensing as viewed from aft: headings increase to the **left**. N at x=0/wrap, E at x=1536, S at x=1024, W at x=512. Cardinal letters every 90°, tens-of-degrees numbers every 30°, ticks every 5°. Seam labels are drawn across both image edges.
- **Static bezel/index overlay:** `gl-front-compass-face.png`, 512 × 512 RGBA. Transparent exterior and rounded transparent window bounded by `(66,202)` to `(446,310)`, with a fixed pale lubber line at x=256. The visible housing bounds are `(28,130)` to `(484,386)`; account for the transparent padding when sizing the plane. This is a compact case front, not the entire cylindrical compass body. Supply body, glass and mounting geometry separately.
- For a flat scrolling card, sample `x = (-heading_deg * 2048/360 + local_x) mod 2048`, with local_x zero at the static lubber line. Equivalently centre U = `(-heading_deg/360) mod 1`, with U increasing toward the pilot's right. Increasing heading moves the card **right** under the line. At heading 0/90/180/270, N/E/S/W must be centred respectively. Do not reverse U again merely because the card is reverse-sensing.
- At native horizontal card scale, the 380 px-wide opening shows about **66.80°**. Fit the band's full 160 px height into the 108 px window height, scaling vertically independently. Image top corresponds to texture v=1 in the existing model convention. Repeat U, clamp V; mask the moving surface to the rounded opening, behind the static overlay. Only the card moves; never rotate the entire face/lubber line with heading. The static pale line must occlude the card and remain visible through the separate glass.
- A cylindrical card may use the same heading-per-U relationship with appropriate geometry UVs; validate sign and zero with the four cardinal tests above. This lane does not alter the rear card texture or its wiring.

All new assets are generated by `make_gl_dials.py`; no external images are sampled or traced. The reference photo is used only for visual comparison. Existing art is deliberately retained, so the audit calls out remaining fidelity gaps for Claude rather than silently changing the rear cockpit's shared textures.

## Generation verification

Generated with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 /usr/bin/python3 tools/own_models/make_gl_dials.py` (Pillow 11.3.0). The default Homebrew Python lacks Pillow; the system Python has it. Existing text-bearing textures render differently on this host because the helper selects an available platform font; avionics faces also encode differently despite identical pixels. After generation, all pre-existing PNGs were restored to their original tracked bytes and checked against pre-run SHA-256 hashes. Only the three new PNGs are delivered; regenerating on another host may likewise require preserving existing textures. No `__pycache__` was created in `tools/own_models`.
