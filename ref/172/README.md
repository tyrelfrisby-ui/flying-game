N77222 provides the original moulded dark panel surround, separate engine gauges, dual yokes, period audio panel and radio proportions. It has later Narco digital radios/transponder and a portable GPS: those are not copied. Altivation is a heavily upgraded aircraft; used for cabin structure, door cards, upholstery seams, dual pedals, trim pedestal and compass mounting, never as a period avionics specification.

Model interpretation: **late 172M-style six-pack with period King equipment**, not a claim to reproduce an untouched 1972 panel. Cessna Owner's restoration article explains that pre-second-half-1975 panels used an offset instrument layout: https://cessnaowner.org/172-restoration172-restoration-part-2-panel/ . The requested six-pack takes precedence. Upholstery is an estimated 1970s saddle/ochre/ivory scheme rather than a serial-specific factory trim match. KX-170B, KR-85-style ADF, separate DME, and KT-76-style transponder are visual period interpretations, not manufacturer replicas or operational avionics.
