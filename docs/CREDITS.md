## Aerobatic sequence cards (2026-10-31)

Extra 300, Pitts S-2B, Great Lakes and the reusable future-Decathlon holder use the **International Aerobatic Club 2026 Power Sportsman Known**, official OpenAero pilot-card diagram (page 4) and Form A catalogue/K values (page 1) from [SPTP-Known-2026.pdf](https://www.iac.org/sites/default/files/Sequences/SPTP-Known-2026.pdf). 11 figures, K 127; 137 including presentation. Added labels and the original static Extra compass use **Liberation Sans Regular/Bold (SIL Open Font License 1.1)**, baked into textures; no fonts ship. Holder/clip/card geometry and compass artwork are original procedural work. See `docs/models/sequence-card-PROVENANCE.md` for source hash, art processing, exact material slots and transforms. The official sequence graphics remain attributed external content.
