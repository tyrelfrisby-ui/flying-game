- **H-4**: a flying boat in runway lessons — "No touchdown" every time. Water lessons, or hide them for the H-4?
- **F-86**: idle-power flares break it; it can't be stalled in the stall lessons (sinks into the ground). Same question as the 737.
- **Gee Bee**: still breaks in the flare lessons (very short-coupled, the stability margin we set just positive).
- **Beaver / Cub on floats** in runway lessons: floats on pavement; hard by nature.
- **Stall lessons**: with a realistic stall-recover pilot, many types drop a wing 90–180° and spiral in over two minutes (the game only helps with ≤10 % aileron/rudder). Extra/Decathlon/Cassutt spin — honest for aerobatic types; Cub/AirCam/Pawnee dropping 80–90° is worth your eye.

- **Glasair**: idle glide 6–6.5:1 at 1,550–1,640 fpm — steep for a slick airframe (windmilling prop drag 0.15 everywhere).
- **EB29R glide lesson** takes > 7.5 min to lose 1,000 ft at 50:1 — consider 500 ft for the open-class.
