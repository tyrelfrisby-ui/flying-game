## The project in one paragraph
iOS flight-training game "Aero Playground" (`com.tyrelfrisby.aeroplayground`). Unity 6000.3.23f1 front end over an
engine-agnostic C# flight sim that is unit-tested with xUnit and compiled into Unity from source. Owner: Tyrel Frisby,
CFI, flies a Cub and gliders. He tests on the device and reports as a pilot ("still too stable", "it should mush at
1,000 ft/min"). He is decisive and short on hands-on time: build, ship, report plainly. Do not survey options at him.

## How to work a pilot complaint (this is what has worked)
1. Reproduce it in a test with a trace (`Trace = true` in `PracticeScenarioTests`, or a probe like
   `PitchMomentBreakdown`, `TrimProbeElevatorNeededVsSpeed`, `ClimbPerformanceBreakdown`, `GroundLoopMomentProbe`).
2. Find the physical cause before touching gains. Three "too stable tailwheel" passes were a mirrored steer frame; the
   8,000 ft/min stall was a CG four inches aft of the leading edge plus a lift cliff in the table; the excess climb was
   flat propeller efficiency.
3. Fix, run the full suite, ship to the phone and TestFlight, commit, tell him what changed in pilot terms.

  (runway lessons and below 100 m AGL). The owner calls this the foundation of the app. Do not add follow damping.
- **Aero** (2026-09-15): Clark Y wing table with post-stall plateau, flat-plate drag and CP shift; real tail lift slope;
  30° elevator; flaps add tail downwash; prop efficiency falls off below design speed. Cub held stall ≈ 1,300 ft/min

## Rules that have bitten before
- Never read `mesh.vertices` in a player build. Keep shader instancing variants ("Keep All").
- Nothing on screen may cover the aircraft; instruments do not move with bank.
- Tests must reset `Atmosphere.SteadyWind` — practice scenarios drive it and it leaks between tests.
- The steer frame in `LandingGear` has + = LEFT. Any code reading `TailwheelState.AngleRad` must use that.
- Stall and approach speeds come from the sim's own trim solver (`EstimateVso`), never from a CLmax guess.
- Side-view lessons are pitch-and-power only (`ConstrainLongitudinal`).
