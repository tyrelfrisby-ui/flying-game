# pitts-s2b-like-own.glb: clean-room Pitts S-2B painted as N31DG (v1, 2026-10-27)

Every mesh, UV and texture in this model is generated by `tools/own_models/build_pitts_s2b.py`, a procedural Blender script that uses the same technique as `build_ea300.py` and `paint.py`. No third-party mesh was downloaded, opened, imported, traced or measured. That covers the FlightGear Pitts S-2B (Kaito, GPL/unclear), the CGTrader, Sketchfab and Dimville models, and the MSFS, Aerofly and X-Plane Pitts. Nothing GPL or paid is in the repo.

Rebuild: `blender -b --python tools/own_models/build_pitts_s2b.py`
Renders: `blender -b --python tools/own_models/pitts_fourview.py -- <glb> <outprefix> [view,...]`

## Paint source: N31DG (S-2B c/n 5071)
- There is only one photo: https://www.pittss2b.com/images/5071.jpg, from https://www.pittss2b.com/5000to5099.htm. Photo by Dave Williams; the owner/operator is Gene Jaros. The copyright is unknown, so the photo is **reference only**. No pixels from it are used. All paint is procedural colour masks plus our own lettering PNGs.
- The airframe was deregistered on 2019-03-18. The N-number now belongs to a 2024 Cessna T182T.
- Fonts used to render `tools/own_models/art/pitts-*.png`:
  - Liberation Sans Bold (SIL OFL 1.1) for "N31DG" and "S-2B".
  - Dancing Script Bold (SIL OFL 1.1) for the "Pitts" script.

### What follows the photo (port side, 3/4 front)
- Signal red base: (200,24,36), the same red as the EA300. White is (246,246,246).
- **Checkered rudder.** The whole rudder aft of the hinge is checkered in 0.098 m (~3.9 in) squares, about 5 columns by 12 rows, counted on the photo crop. There is a red check at the top-forward corner. The fin ahead of the hinge is plain red, with a white "Pitts" script and "S-2B" under it.
- **Wing tops.** The upper and lower wings have a white/red zig-zag sunburst with white tips.
- **Stab.** The stab top has white rays fanning from the root LE, carried across the elevators.
- **Fuselage side.** A white flash runs over the cowl front and sweeps into twin pinstripes that end below the rear cockpit.
- **Registration.** "N31DG" is in white block capitals about 13 in tall, on the aft fuselage on both sides. It reads forward on both.
- **Other parts.** The struts, gear legs, pants and spinner are red. The wires are steel. The prop is black.

### What is assumed: not visible in the photo
- **Checkered wing undersides**, as Ty asked: "checkered rudder and bottom of wings". All four lower surfaces, ailerons included, carry a full red/white checkerboard in 10 in squares (4 across the 40 in chord) on the wing's span/chord grid. The real layout, square size and coverage are unknown.
- The stab underside and the belly are plain red.
- The starboard side mirrors the port side.
- The prop type and spinner colour are inferred. One black blade is seen horizontal, which is consistent with a 2-blade prop, but the TCDS also lists 3-blade options. The 4 in white tips are an assumption.

## Known gaps / differences
- Paint comes from a single low-resolution photo. The underside checks are assumed (see above).
- Dimensions not in a published spec (sweep, stagger, gap, fuselage stations, tail outlines) are measured off a brochure three-view, ±2 in.
- The airfoils are generic. The incidence is 1 deg on both wings, against Jane's 1.5 deg (decalage 0 either way).
- The AEIO-540 is simplified (case, cylinders, heads, ducts). It has no baffles, mags or plumbing, and only two exhaust stubs.
- The slave struts are separate objects and are not bound to the aileron rotation.
- The canopy is a single bubble with 3 bows. There are no hinges, no pilots and no placards.
- **Not hooked into Unity yet.** The GLB is not referenced by any config or OwnSpecs entry, and in-game binding (AirframeVisual.CarveModel regions) is untested. The .meta files were written by hand (new GUIDs), copied from the EA300 GLB importer settings.
- This branch is based on `codex/own-ea300` (it needs `tools/own_models/paint.py`).

## v2 (owner review of v1, 2026-10-27)
All changes were made in `tools/own_models/build_pitts_s2b.py`, and the GLB was regenerated from it. It is still entirely original geometry generated by script, with no third-party meshes.
- **Paint:** both wings carry the sunburst/zig-zag on top. Only the underside of the **lower** wing is checkerboard. The underside of the upper wing (and its ailerons) is plain red. This replaces the v1 assumption.
- **Main gear:** a steel-tube A-frame on each side, much wider at the fuselage. The front and rear legs pick up at the lower longerons (x +0.47 / −0.21 m, y ±0.22 m) and converge at the axle. Two streamlined struts run from the inboard lower gear leg, abeam the axle, to the belly midpoint. They end at a red belly blister (`Gear_Bungee_Housing`) with a bungee spool and cord wraps (`Gear_Bungee`) that provide the shock absorption. The pants and wheels are unchanged. Pick-up stations were measured off the brochure three-view (front and side), ±2 in.
- **Wingtips:** a half-circle planform (radius = half chord, 20 in) on both wings. The ailerons extend 0.25 m into the tip arc, and the slave strut moved from y 2.62 to 2.50 m.
- **Rudder:** the outline is reshaped so it is fullest at or just below the stabiliser and narrower toward the top. The v1 shape was the reverse.
- **Prop:** the pitch/twist handedness now matches American rotation, clockwise seen from the cockpit as on a Lycoming. The blade chord's leading edge is in the direction `+x × radial`. Blades are grey (128,131,136) with 4 in white tips.
- **Nose stripes:** the two white stripes on each side form a symmetric pair. Going aft, the upper one angles up and the lower one angles down by the same amount, opening from 1.4 in apart at the cowl front to 6.3 in apart at x 0.78 m. From there they run parallel to the pointed ends. A white diamond (13 in long) sits between them. This follows the owner's description and the brochure three-view. The single N31DG photo is too low-resolution to confirm the diamond.

Size: 38.5k tris (38,492), 1.63 MB.

## v3 (2026-10-27)

All geometry and textures are still generated procedurally by `tools/own_models/build_pitts_s2b.py`. No third-party or GPL meshes, textures or fonts were added. Reference photos were used only by eye.

- **Rudder:** the outline is a 50/50 blend of the v1 and v2 trailing-edge tables (`RTE = 0.5*(RTE_V1 + RTE_V2)`, sampled per z).
- **Belly:** the lower fuselage station heights are raised by up to ~4 cm amidships, and the aft taper is straightened. This removes the pot belly and the recurve.
- **Gear fairing:** a new `Gear_Fairing` object adds a red fabric panel per side, filling the A-frame between the front and rear gear legs. It is a thin lens-section skin, like the real S-2B gear-leg fairing.
- **Gear geometry:** the front legs, the streamlined belly (bungee) struts and the bungee housing now all sit in the transverse plane x = 0.476 m. The bungee station moved from its v2 position to `X_BG = X_AX`.
- **Top-wing paint:** the top of the upper wing has a conventional sunburst. White and red rays radiate from the wing centre, widening to the tips and trailing edge (boundaries at 12°, 2°, −4° and −22° from a virtual hub at the wing centreline). The top of the lower wing keeps the v2 zigzag. Both wing undersides are unchanged.
- **Fuselage stripes:** the stripes follow Ty's style-guide photo of a Horizon Hobby RC Pitts. The upper stripe sweeps down and aft from the cowl top and then runs level. The lower stripe is nearly level, with a white diamond on the nose side between them. Only the stripe layout was copied by eye. The livery is still N31DG (N-number, Pitts script, checkered rudder), and none of the model's registration or branding was used.
- **Size:** 40,276 triangles; GLB 1.66 MB.

## v4 (2026-10-27): factory sunburst scheme, two liveries, three-view tail

Builds: `PITTS_LIVERY=N31DG` (default) writes `pitts-s2b-like-own.glb`; `PITTS_LIVERY=N1191` writes
`pitts-s2b-like-own-n1191.glb`. Geometry is identical; paint and prop differ. Command:
`PITTS_LIVERY=N1191 blender -b --python tools/own_models/build_pitts_s2b.py`. Triangle counts: 52,696 (N1191), 51,404 (N31DG).
Bounds: 5.724 m long, 6.094 m span, 2.175 m tall (wheels to fin top).

### Paint references: used by eye only
The Aviat factory paint scheme was laid out by eye from these published painted three-views, mainly the first two:
Horizons NZ Model Graphics S-2B sheet, AirshowModels 1/32 S-2B manual page 8, the Munden S-2C sheet (cross-check only),
and a photo of a green S-2B (pattern cross-check). **Nothing was traced, sampled or copied into a texture.** Every
boundary is our own simple geometry, generated in `build_pitts_s2b.py`:
- straight sloped stripes;
- quadratic-Bezier swooshes;
- diamonds as |dx|/a + |dz|/b < 1;
- sunburst rays, given as angle sectors from a hub.

All scheme elements are recreated this way:
- **Base colour:** red (200, 24, 36).
- **Outlines:** every white area has a 16 mm navy (22, 26, 62) outline. Lettering and checkerboards are not outlined.
- **Upper wing top:**
  - red leading-edge band (13 cm, follows the rounded tips);
  - red centre V;
  - white/red sunburst rays fanning aft and outboard from a hub at |y| 0.38 m behind the band. Red sectors are 12–26°, 38–52°, 62–71° and 77–81.5° from straight aft.
- **Upper wing underside:** plain red.
- **Lower wing top:** the same sunburst without the LE band. The ASM sheet labels this "BOTTOM WING SUNBURST PATTERN", and Munden agrees. The steering note said to judge this from the drawings, which overrides the earlier "top of the bottom wing solid red".
- **Lower wing underside:**
  - N1191: a white spanwise stripe at 9–20 % chord, plus three white diamonds per side at mid-chord (factory).
  - N31DG: red/white checkerboard, as in v3.
- **Stab and elevators:** rays from a hub at the stab root LE (|y| 0.205 m). The sectors match the rays printed on the Aviat factory three-view: red 5–21°, 34–49° and ≥ 61°. The underside is red. See `pitts-s2b-v4-tail-planform-overlay.png`.
- **Fuselage:**
  - two white stripes from the nose diamond to the rudder post;
  - upper and lower swooshes from the diamond to the cowl top and cowl bottom;
  - a white band over the cowl top and a white chevron under the cowl;
  - a white turtledeck wedge behind the canopy, from the canopy rear to x = −2.20 m.
- **Wheel pants:** a white nose diamond and two thin stripes on each pant.
- **Rudder:**
  - N1191: three white diamonds;
  - N31DG: the red/white checkerboard from v3.
- **Fin:** "Pitts" script and "S-2B" (as v3).
- **Registration:** 25 cm white letters on the aft fuselage under the stripes. The N1191 artwork (`art/pitts-n1191.png`) is set in Liberation Sans Bold, the same as N31DG.
- **Props:** both are clockwise seen from the cockpit, and both use the TCDS options.
  - N1191: MT-Propeller 3-blade composite, 74.8 in, wide paddle blades, white with red tips (5 in).
  - N31DG: Hartzell 2-blade, 80 in, grey with white tips.
- **Spinner:** red on both. HNZ draws it silver; we kept red to match the N31DG photo.

## v5 (2026-10-27): pointed elevators, V gap and rudder travel clearance

Owner request: the elevator halves should come to a point at the rudder post/hinge line, with a V-shaped gap between them so the rudder can swing through its full TCDS travel plus at least 1 inch of clearance.

### Source: travel limits
- FAA Type Certificate Data Sheet **A8SO**, Revision 21 (February 15, 2005), Model S-2B section (p. 9), "Movements":
  - Rudder: LEFT 30° ± .75°, RIGHT 30° ± .75°.
  - Elevator: UP 27° ± .75°, DOWN 27° ± .75°. Elevator tab: UP 7° / DOWN 19° ± 2° (not modelled).
- Checked at the tolerance limit: rudder **30.75°** each way, elevator **±27.75°**.

### Geometry changes (both liveries, from `build_pitts_s2b.py`)
- **Elevators:**
  - Each half's inboard edge is a straight diagonal cut from a point on the hinge line at |y| = 0.082 m, running aft and outboard at 30° to the hinge line (`E_IN = 0.082`, `E_PHI = 30°`).
  - The cut meets the elevator TE at |y| = 0.358 m (`E_MEET`).
  - Seen from above, the halves form a V that opens aft, with its apex at the rudder post.
- **Rudder:**
  - Added a half-round nose (local half-thickness radius) centred on the hinge line. Its profile is the same at any deflection, so the gap at the hinge stays constant.
  - The lower aft part needed no reshaping. Its bottom sits below the elevators' reach and clears the tail spring and tailwheel by ≥ 52 mm.
- **Fin:** the aft face moved forward to the hinge line + local post radius + 3 mm, so the swinging rudder never enters it (v4: hinge line + 4 mm).
- **Stab:**
  - The centre section (|y| < 0.045 m) now ends at the hinge line + 26 mm, so it sits clear of the rudder nose.
  - It blends back to the hinge line + 4 mm by |y| = 0.065 m.

### Verification (`tools/own_models/pitts_rudder_clearance.py` on the exported GLBs)
The script rotates the Rudder object about the hinge and both elevators about their hinge. It then measures the minimum distance between surfaces (vertex to surface, both ways) and runs a BVH triangle-intersection test.

| Check (rudder 30.75° L and R) | N31DG | N1191 |
|---|---|---|
| Rudder TE/bottom to near elevator inboard edge, elevator neutral | 35.8 mm (1.41 in) | 35.8 mm |
| Same, worst elevator deflection (±27.75°) | **30.6 mm (1.20 in)** | 30.6 mm |
| Rudder to far elevator | ≥ 50.3 mm | ≥ 50.3 mm |
| First rudder–elevator contact angle | 37.6° (elev 0), 40.3° (elev −27.75°), 41.2° (elev +27.75°) | same |
| Fin / stab / fuselage / flying wires / tail gear | 3.2 / 19.9 / 3.1 / 167.8 / 52.8 mm, 0 intersecting triangles | same |

- Requirement: ≥ 25.4 mm (1 in) at full travel. It passes with 5.2 mm of margin in the worst case.
- For comparison, v4 contacted the elevators at 24.6° rudder, below the TCDS travel, and clipped the fin and stab.
