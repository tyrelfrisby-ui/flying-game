Conventions (same as the 2-33 / Extra): glTF frame (Y up; nose toward -X, so +X = aft, +Y = up, +Z = port, the pilot's left). Every moving node has its origin on its hinge/pivot, so `kind: rot` = rotate the node's local rest pose by `angle_deg = interp(map, param)` about `axis_gltf` (unit vector, model space at rest, right-hand rule); `kind: move` = translate by `interp(map)` metres along `axis_gltf`. Maps are piecewise linear and clamp at the ends, except nodes with extra `rig_map_extrapolate` (altimeter hands: unbounded) and `rig_map_wrap` 360 (compass card). Every node carries the same data as glTF extras (`rig_param`, `rig_axis_gltf` or `rig_move_axis_gltf`, `rig_map_param`, `rig_map_deg` / `rig_map_m`, `rig_param_model`, `rig_desc`). The hinged flight surfaces (Aileron_UL/UR/LL/LR_Pivot, Elevator_L/R_Pivot, Rudder_Pivot) also carry `hinge_axis_marker` (= `<surface>_HingeAxis`, an empty at the far end of the hinge line, so axis = marker − pivot) and `hinge_axis_gltf`. Child nodes are already parented in the GLB: apply LOCAL rotations (Stick_F/R ride on TorqueTube; Elevator_L/R_Pivot ride on Stab_TrimPivot; needles ride on Instruments_Front/Rear).

Hierarchy: `Cockpit` (group) > `Instruments_Front`, `Instruments_Rear`, `Controls` (TorqueTube > Stick_F, Stick_R; Pedal_*, Brake_*, Throttle/Prop/Mixture_*, Trim_*, FuelValve_*), `Interior` (Cockpit_Interior = all static cockpit parts in one multi-material mesh, Cockpit_Liner = inner skin), `Eye_Front`, `Eye_Rear` (camera points). Tail: `Stab_TrimPivot` > Stab, `Elevator_L_Pivot` > Elevator_L, `Elevator_R_Pivot` > Elevator_R; `Rudder_Pivot` > Rudder. Wings: `Aileron_<s>_Pivot` > `Aileron_<s>` (v11/v12, unchanged), Strut_Slave_L/R with pin markers. The mesh names Aileron_*, Elevator_L/R and Rudder are unchanged, so AirframeVisual's `namedSurfaces` carving keeps working as before (the new pivots are empties, no mesh).

## Params (all from the sim; front and rear cockpits always show the same value)
- Controls: `aileron` -1..+1 (+1 roll right: stick tops right, left ailerons TE down, right TE up), `elevator` -1..+1 (+1 stick aft, elevator TE up), `rudder` -1..+1 (+1 right pedal forward, rudder TE to starboard), `brake_left` / `brake_right` 0..1 (heel brakes, 1 = pushed), `trim` -1..+1 (+1 = knob aft = nose up = stabilizer LE down), `throttle`, `prop`, `mixture` 0..1 (0 = lever full aft / idle / low rpm / idle cut-off, 1 = full forward), `fuel_valve` 0..1 (1 = ON, 0 = OFF), `magneto` 0..4 (0 OFF, 1 R, 2 L, 3 BOTH, 4 START; detents, START springs back to 3).
- Instruments: `airspeed_mph`, `altitude_ft` (unbounded), `vsi_fpm` (±2000), `rpm`, `manifold_inhg`, `oil_press_psi`, `oil_temp_f`, `cht_f`, `heading_deg` (card drum; reads like a real wet compass: higher headings appear to the LEFT of the lubber line), `turn_rate_dps` (±6; 3 = standard rate index), `slip_ball` -1..+1 (+1 = ball to the pilot's right), `g_load` (-4..+10), `g_max_seen` / `g_min_seen` (accelerometer tell-tales: the sim keeps the running max / min and a reset; front and rear G-meters show the same values).
- Avionics (rear panel, v13.1): `com_freq_mhz` (118.000..136.990, active), `com_standby_mhz`, `com_volume` 0..1, `xpdr_squawk` (4-digit octal, e.g. 7000), `xpdr_mode` (OFF / SBY / ON / ALT), `xpdr_knob_clicks` (integer, free-running). The knobs are rig nodes (table); the frequency / squawk TEXT is drawn by the sim on the display nodes (Displays, below).
- Stabilizer trim: the stabilizer is MODELLED at 0 deg = trim 1.0714 (0.25 deg past full nose-up), so the sim must always drive `Stab_TrimPivot` (trim 0 = LE up 3.75 deg). Sense: nose-up trim = stabilizer LE down (confirmed by Ty, 2026-10-30).
- Ailerons: drive the four pivots from `aileron` with the maps above; the lower ailerons are the drivers, the uppers are the solved constant-strut-length followers (v12), and the slave struts re-aim from Strut_Slave_<s>_PinLower to _PinUpper (see PROVENANCE v11/v12).
- Coupled parts: TorqueTube carries Stick_F + Stick_R (roll); Stick_F/R share `elevator`; all four Pedal_* share `rudder` (left pair moves opposite the right pair); Brake_F/R_L share `brake_left`, Brake_F/R_R `brake_right`; Throttle/Prop/Mixture/Trim/FuelValve _F and _R share their param. Mag switch, VSI, turn coordinator, MP, CHT, Hobbs, compass and the radio / transponder heads are on the rear (PIC) panel only, as in the photos; the front panel has altimeter, ASI, G-meter (v13.1, Ty), tach and the oil pressure / temperature gauge.
