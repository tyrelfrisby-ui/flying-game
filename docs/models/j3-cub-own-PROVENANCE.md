## Clean-room statement
Every vertex is generated by `build_j3_cub.py` from numbers measured by eye off the references below, using a pixel grid
and typed tables. No third-party mesh, UV, texture or model file is read, imported or traced. No logo, script, "Cub"
bear or registration is painted: the default livery is Cub yellow with a plain black lightning stripe.

## Variant modelled
- **Engine and prop:**
  - J-3C-65 with the Continental A-65 cylinder heads sticking out of the cowl under black sheet-metal hoods, with the
    left bank forward (Continental).
  - Exhaust stacks, nose-bowl inlets, and the fuel cap with its float wire.
  - 72 in fixed-pitch wood prop at 42 in pitch, no spinner, turning clockwise from the cockpit.
- **Right-side split door,** as separate hinged parts:
  - `DoorUpper_R` (with `Window_R`) folds UP under the wing: +90°.
  - `DoorLower_R` folds DOWN against the side: −175°.
- **Wheels:**
  - 8.00-4 low-pressure tyres on 4 in wheels.
  - Bungee (shock-cord) main gear with leather boots.
  - Leaf-spring tailwheel with a steerable head and steering springs to the rudder horns.
- **Floats:**
  - EDO 60-1320 floats with aluminium struts, spreader bars, and spreader and pull wires.
  - ONE water rudder, on the LEFT float only (as asked; it matches the 60-1320 parts list, where the RH float comes
    "less water rudder"), steered by cables from the rudder horn.
  - No tailwheel and no ventral fin.

## Rig (both GLBs)
Node origins sit on the hinge lines, axles and pivots. The axes are glTF vectors, stored in the extras:

| Part | Extras |
|---|---|
| `Aileron_L/_R`, `Elevator_L/_R`, `Rudder` | `hinge_axis_gltf` |
| `Prop`, `Wheel_L/_R`, `Wheel_Tail` | `spin_axis_gltf` |
| `TailWheel_Steer` | `steer_axis_gltf` |
| `DoorUpper_R` / `DoorLower_R` | `hinge_axis_gltf` + `open_deg` (90 / −175; 0 = closed) |
| `Gear_Pivot_L/_R` (empties `Gear_HingeAxis_*`, `Gear_ShockTop_*`) | `hinge_deg_full` 9.02, `wheel_rise_full_m` 0.10, `strut_len_extended_m` 1.0335, `strut_extension_full_m` 0.0333, `wheel_up_sign_model_frame` |
| `WaterRudder_L` (floats only) | `steer_axis_gltf` |
| `WaterRudder_Blade_L` (child; floats only) | `retract_axis_gltf`, `retract_deg` 90 (0 = down in the water) |

- `Gear_Pivot_L/_R` rotate the legs, wheel and lower strut tube about the Pivot→HingeAxis line.
- The bungee strut **extends** as the wheel rises, because the cords work in tension.
- `Float_L` carries the `edo1320_*` extras.

## Judgement calls / open
- Rocker-hood size and the windshield-to-cowl join at the upper corner were set by eye (slightly heavy in close-ups).
- The aileron chord is about 0.355 m against the config's 0.388.
- The prop pitch (42 in) is a choice inside the TCDS range.
- Gear travel is not published; 0.10 m wheel rise is nominal.
- The float step (drawing station xd 2.144) sits about 0.24–0.47 m aft of the EDO CG band (xd 1.67–1.905) at
